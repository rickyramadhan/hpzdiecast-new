Page index

Every template in the mockup, grouped the way a customer moves through the store. The chips under each page open useful states directly — filters, a casting, a specific product.

Demo tips

  • ?resetClears demo state — cart, wishlist, garage, orders, points.
  • ?staticDisables animations — for screenshots and slow machines.
  • EN · USDRegion / language switch in the top bar — prices, shipping, duties and payment methods change with it.
  • /Press / anywhere to open predictive search.
  • My garagePick a casting in the garage bar — every product card then tags parts that fit.
